Handling waste and excavated material.

Sorting, reuse and traceability

Groundwork can produce large amounts of topsoil, sand, gravel, asphalt, concrete, timber and other materials. The right handling depends on the type and condition of the material and its intended use. P. Blöndal ehf. aims to avoid unnecessary waste, maximise lawful reuse and ensure that material that must be disposed of goes to an appropriate reception facility. Clean soil in particular must be kept separate from suspect or contaminated material.

Assessing excavated material

Before material is removed from a site, we assess whether it can be reused in the job, in another approved project, or whether it counts as waste. Reuse must comply with the law, permits, the properties of the material and technical requirements. It must not be assumed that all excavated material is automatically clean or suitable. The destination and responsibility for transport are agreed with the client.

Sorting on site

Waste is separated by type and according to current sorting requirements; common categories include stone and masonry, metals, timber, plastic, glass, plasterboard and hazardous waste where it occurs. Mixed waste can make recycling harder and increase costs. Hazardous substances, oil filters and oil-contaminated absorbents need special handling and delivery. Staff must know which materials must not be mixed with others.

Suspected contamination

If unusual odours, discolouration, an oil film, old tanks or unknown substances appear, further spreading is stopped and an assessment by a qualified professional is requested. The material is not taken to a general soil reception site until its status has been established and the necessary requirements met. Responsibility for investigations, permits and disposal must be clarified between the client and the contractor.

Transport and records

As the job and the rules require, records are kept of quantities, type of material, transport, the receiving facility and relevant delivery receipts. Overloading, securing of loads and cleaning of equipment are monitored where relevant. At completion, we make sure that no waste piles or polluting materials are left behind without an agreed arrangement.

Responsibility and review

The managing director is responsible for this document and for making sure its content is presented to everyone working on the company's behalf. It is reviewed when there are significant changes to the work, equipment or rules, after incidents, and otherwise regularly in line with the scale of the business.
