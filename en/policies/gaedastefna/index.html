Quality policy.

Reliable work and careful finishing

P. Blöndal ehf. aims to deliver work that meets the contract requirements, approved drawings and the client's expectations. Quality in groundwork is about both what can be seen and what disappears below the surface: soil replacement, compaction, pipe beds, levels and falls for drainage. Mistakes that are covered up before they are discovered can be costly. Quality control must therefore follow a job from the first request to final handover.

Clear assumptions

Before work begins, the scope, boundaries, drawings, quantities, access, choice of materials, deadlines and the way extra work is approved must be confirmed. Unclear instructions are raised with the client before they lead to the wrong result. If actual ground conditions differ from the design, this is reported, and critical assumptions are not changed without the approval of the responsible party.

Quality in groundwork

When digging and filling, levels, slopes, stability, type of material and finishing must be under control. Where compaction matters, the approved material specification and the testing specified in the contract are followed; a visual check alone does not prove a particular degree of compaction. For utility work, the pipe owner's own requirements for bedding, protective layers, warning tape and surveying also apply.

Surface finishing

For paving, the sub-base, proper compaction, correct falls for drainage, stable edging and a high-quality surface must be ensured. For turf and green areas, the soil, levels and drainage must be assessed. It is important to compare the state of the work with expectations before covering parts of the work that will later be inaccessible.

Deviations, changes and handover

If a deviation is found, its extent is assessed, the affected part of the work is stopped when necessary, and the correction is decided in consultation with the right party. Photos, measurements and approvals are kept in proportion to the size and risk of the project. At completion, finishing, clean-up, access and any outstanding items are reviewed with the client. Quality also means handing over the right information, not just a finished surface.

Responsibility and review

The managing director is responsible for this document and for making sure its content is presented to everyone working on the company's behalf. It is reviewed when there are significant changes to the work, equipment or rules, after incidents, and otherwise regularly in line with the scale of the business.
