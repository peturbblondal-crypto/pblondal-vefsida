In groundwork and surface finishing, the main environmental impacts relate to disturbance of land, transport of materials, waste, noise, dust and the risk of leaks from machinery. P. Blöndal ehf. aims to carry out work with as little disturbance as possible and to leave sites in the agreed condition. Environmental considerations are reviewed from the start of each job and kept in mind when the work changes.

Preventive planning

At the start of a job we check restrictions on the work area, nearby drains, vegetation, watercourses, soil and possible sources of pollution. We agree with the client where soil may be stored, where machines may be driven and where equipment may be cleaned. Where soil is suspected of being contaminated, unsafe handling is stopped and a professional assessment is requested before the material is moved or reused.

Oils, fuel and hazardous substances

During refuelling and maintenance the aim is to prevent leaks onto the ground and into drains. Spill kits must be available according to the risk of the job, and staff must know how to respond. Damage to hoses, hydraulic systems and tanks is repaired before work continues. Hazardous waste goes to an appropriate reception facility, not into general waste.

Noise, dust and housekeeping

Work is planned so that idling and unnecessary machine movements are kept to a minimum. On dry days we assess whether dust suppression is needed, paying particular attention to nearby homes, schools and other sensitive activities. Access routes are kept as clean as possible, and soil, concrete washout and fine material must not reach drains or spread beyond the defined work area.

Follow-up and improvement

When a job is finished, surfaces, vegetation and clean-up are checked against the contract. Environmental incidents and complaints are investigated with a focus on cause and prevention. Management decides whether procedures need to change, whether equipment is adequate and whether further training is needed. Decisions on handling waste and soil are based on the rules in force at each location.

Responsibility and review

The managing director is responsible for this document and for making sure its content is presented to everyone working on the company's behalf. It is reviewed when there are significant changes to the work, equipment or rules, after incidents, and otherwise regularly in line with the scale of the business.
