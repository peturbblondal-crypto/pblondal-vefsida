Equality and equal pay policy.

Equal rights, objective decisions

P. Blöndal ehf. aims for everyone who works for the company to have fair opportunities and be treated with respect. In a practical trade, where roles can vary and there are many partners, it is important that equality covers recruitment, pay, training, allocation of tasks, facilities and communication. Decisions must never be based on irrelevant considerations.

Recruitment and development

When selecting staff, skills, experience, licences and the ability to do the job are the legitimate criteria. Staff have equal access to training, information, opportunities and responsibility according to the nature of their work. Facilities are organised so that no unnecessary barriers arise because of gender, disability or other personal circumstances. Where only a few people work together, the manager must take particular care that informal habits do not lead to discrimination.

Pay and terms of employment

Pay and pay changes are based on clear, objective criteria such as responsibility, the nature of the work, education, licences, experience and workload. The same work, or work of equal value, receives equal pay without gender-based discrimination. The company follows the rules on equal pay, record-keeping and disclosure that apply to its size and activities at any given time.

A safe and respectful workplace

Bullying, gender-based or sexual harassment, violence and degrading behaviour are not tolerated. Reports are handled objectively and as confidentially as possible. A person who is affected or who raises a concern must not face retaliation. Where a manager may be involved in a case, it must be possible to turn to another appropriate person or obtain independent advice.

Follow-up and review

As recruitment, responsibilities and pay levels grow, the company reviews whether job titles are comparable and whether decisions are supported by objective reasons. Pay and personal information are kept confidential when equality is reviewed. The policy is reviewed when the business or the law changes; adapting properly to the size of the company matters more than adopting the systems of large corporations unchanged.

Responsibility and review

The managing director is responsible for this document and for making sure its content is presented to everyone working on the company's behalf. It is reviewed when there are significant changes to the work, equipment or rules, after incidents, and otherwise regularly in line with the scale of the business.
