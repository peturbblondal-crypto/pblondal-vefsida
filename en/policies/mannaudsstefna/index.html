The success of a small groundwork company depends above all on how people work together, use their experience and take responsibility for their own safety and the quality of their work. P. Blöndal ehf. aims to be a workplace where clear communication, professionalism and fairness are a natural part of everyday work. The scope of HR matters is adapted to the size of the company without compromising on fundamental rights.

Recruitment and terms of employment

Before work begins, the role, type of employment, pay and responsibilities must be clear. Relevant laws and collective agreements are followed, and pay, holidays, rest periods and insurance are provided according to the rules. Hiring is based on skills, relevant licences, experience and actual need. On projects managed by others, it must be clearly defined who directs the work and who is responsible for coordination.

Skills and knowledge

No one may operate a machine or carry out specialist tasks without the necessary skills and licences. New staff receive an introduction to the equipment, risks, personal protective equipment, emergency procedures and groundwork methods. Training depends on the task: digging near utility lines, driving and transport, traffic management, compaction and surface finishing all require different knowledge. Staff receive instructions when equipment or methods change.

Communication and wellbeing

We value honest communication, respect and the right to raise concerns without fear of negative consequences. Bullying, harassment, violence and discrimination are not tolerated. Work is organised to reduce excessive fatigue, working in isolation and unclear division of tasks. During busy periods, safety and quality requirements still apply, even when the schedule is tight.

Management and feedback

The managing director or the person responsible for a project holds regular conversations about progress, safety incidents, training needs and workload. Staff's practical knowledge is used to improve equipment and planning. When disagreements arise, solutions are sought objectively and confidentially. HR matters are reviewed when the number of staff or the nature of the business changes.

Responsibility and review

The managing director is responsible for this document and for making sure its content is presented to everyone working on the company's behalf. It is reviewed when there are significant changes to the work, equipment or rules, after incidents, and otherwise regularly in line with the scale of the business.
