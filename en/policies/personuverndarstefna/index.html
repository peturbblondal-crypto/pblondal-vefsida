A groundwork company handles information about clients, staff, contacts, suppliers and, where relevant, people who appear in photos from work sites. P. Blöndal ehf. processes personal data lawfully, fairly and transparently, in accordance with the Icelandic Data Protection Act no. 90/2018.

Data controller

P. Blöndal ehf., company ID 570125-0950, is the data controller for the processing described in this policy. Questions and requests about data protection can be sent to petur@pblondal.is or made by phone on 773-3363.

Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed for enquiries and quotes, contracts, invoicing, statutory bookkeeping, recruitment, payroll and safety on site. Processing is always based on an appropriate legal basis, such as a contract, a legal obligation or legitimate interests. Data is not collected for vague purposes and is not kept longer than necessary.

Enquiries through the website

When you send an enquiry through the form on pblondal.is, we receive your name, phone number, email address, job location, type of job, preferred timing and the description you write. This information is used only to answer your enquiry and prepare a quote, at your request. The form is sent to us by email through the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co), and the company's email is hosted by Google Workspace. The information is not used for marketing, and it is not sold or passed on to others for their own use.

The website

The website does not use cookies or any analytics or advertising tools. If you choose a light or dark theme, your choice is stored in your browser and is not sent to us. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for technical purposes. The website's fonts are hosted on the site itself and are not loaded from third parties.

Photos, cameras and work sites

Photos of work in progress are useful for quality records and traceability, but should focus on the work rather than on people. Identifiable photos of staff, clients or third parties are not published in marketing material without a lawful basis and appropriate information. Data protection is also considered when recording vehicles, location data and communication with subcontractors.

Access, retention and sharing

Access to documents, payroll information and contact lists is limited to those who need the information for their work. Accounting records, such as invoices, are kept for seven years from the end of the financial year, as required by the Icelandic Bookkeeping Act; other data is kept only as long as necessary. When service providers process data for the company, for example for email, bookkeeping or the web form, we make sure appropriate agreements and terms are in place. Passwords and backups are kept secure.

Your rights

You have the right to know what information we process about you and to access it, and where applicable to have it corrected, deleted or its processing restricted, as provided by law. Requests can be sent to petur@pblondal.is. If you believe that processing breaks the law, you can file a complaint with the Icelandic Data Protection Authority, Persónuvernd (personuvernd.is).

Data breaches

If a data breach occurs, the leak is stopped, the impact is assessed, and the Data Protection Authority and those affected are notified when the law requires it.

Responsibility and review

The managing director is responsible for this document and for making sure its content is presented to everyone working on the company's behalf. It is reviewed when there are significant changes to the work, equipment or rules, after incidents, and otherwise regularly in line with the scale of the business.
