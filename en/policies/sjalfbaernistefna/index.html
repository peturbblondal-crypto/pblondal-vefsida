With this policy, P. Blöndal ehf. lays the foundation for a sustainable contracting business in which people's safety, care for the environment and sound business practice go hand in hand. The company carries out groundwork, excavation, utility work and site finishing, where even a small decision on site can affect nearby pipes and cables, the soil, neighbours and the client's costs. Sustainability should be a practical part of planning every job, not just a statement on a website.

Purpose and scope

The policy covers planning, purchasing, machine use, soil transport, people, communication with clients and handover of work. It applies to managers and everyone working on the company's behalf. In a small company the focus must be on a few clear procedures that are easy to follow, rather than a complicated reporting system.

Environmental responsibility

The company aims to reduce unnecessary trips, idling and fuel use through careful planning. When soil is replaced, we first assess whether clean excavated material can be reused, subject to the required permits and the client's approval. When buying equipment and materials we look at durability, availability of spare parts, running costs and reuse. Pollution from oil, concrete washout or other substances is prevented as far as possible.

People and community

Sustainability also covers the health and safety of staff, fair working conditions, equal opportunities and honest communication. On site we pay attention to pedestrians, access for neighbours, and keeping dust, noise and soil transport to a minimum. We only take on work that the company has the skills, equipment and capacity to carry out safely.

Practical follow-up

The company monitors incidents and near misses, fuel use, waste, reuse of soil and other materials, and feedback from clients. The first step is to establish a baseline, and the company only publishes figures on reductions or carbon performance when they are based on traceable measurements. Results are assessed in a simple annual business review and improvements are decided for the next period.

Responsibility and review

The managing director is responsible for this document and for making sure its content is presented to everyone working on the company's behalf. It is reviewed when there are significant changes to the work, equipment or rules, after incidents, and otherwise regularly in line with the scale of the business.
