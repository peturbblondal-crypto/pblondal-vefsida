P. Blöndal ehf.'s business relationships are built on reliability, respect and transparency. This code of conduct describes how the company deals with clients, staff, subcontractors, suppliers, neighbours and public authorities. A good reputation is built not only on finishing jobs but also on how mistakes, changes and disagreements are handled.

Honesty and professionalism

Quotes, invoices, time records and progress reports must be accurate and traceable. The company does not charge for work that has not been done, does not hide known defects and does not imply that it holds licences, equipment or certifications it does not have. If circumstances change and the cost or timeframe increases, the client is informed as soon as possible and approval is sought for the changes.

Conflicts of interest and gifts

When buying materials or choosing subcontractors, decisions are based on price, quality, competence and safety rather than personal gain. Gifts, fees or payments must not improperly influence business decisions. Where personal relationships may affect a decision, the relevant parties are informed and the matter is handled impartially.

Communication and respect

Staff and partners behave courteously, even when work is under pressure. When working in residential areas, we show consideration for neighbours, pedestrians and others affected. Disagreements about finishing, damage or quantities are resolved with records, conversation and correction if a mistake has been made. Threats, discrimination and inappropriate behaviour are not tolerated.

Confidentiality and responsibility

Drawings, client documents, prices, contact details and information about staff are handled according to their purpose and duty of confidentiality. Safety and legality come before business interests. Anyone who becomes aware of a breach of the rules should report it to a manager or another appropriate person; the aim is to respond and learn, not to cover up.

Responsibility and review

The managing director is responsible for this document and for making sure its content is presented to everyone working on the company's behalf. It is reviewed when there are significant changes to the work, equipment or rules, after incidents, and otherwise regularly in line with the scale of the business.
