Health and safety policy.

People's safety comes first

No stage of a job is so urgent that it justifies putting people's health or lives at risk. P. Blöndal ehf. aims for a working culture in which hazards are discussed before work begins, staff have the authority to stop unsafe work, and incidents lead to real improvements. Particular attention is paid to machinery, trenches, traffic, utility cables, fall hazards, noise and working alone.

Risk assessment and planning

When planning a job, we review the hazards on site, ground conditions, nearby pipes and cables, traffic, routes for machinery and access for other contractors. A written health and safety plan and an appropriate risk assessment must be in place as required. The risk assessment is updated when conditions change and reviewed before work on a new hazard begins.

Machinery and trenches

Machines are operated only by competent people holding the licences required. Machines and equipment are checked before use. People must be kept clear of the bucket's working area, the slewing area and any possible fall or tip-over zone. No one enters a trench unless its stability, access, risk of collapse and appropriate protection have been assessed and secured. The need for battering, shoring or other measures depends on the conditions and the rules.

Health, protection and communication

Personal protective equipment is chosen according to the risk, such as safety boots, helmet, high-visibility clothing, hearing protection and eye protection. Dust, vibration, cold, ice and fatigue must also be addressed. When working at remote sites, reliable means of communication must be in place, along with a response if someone does not answer. First aid and information on emergency help must be available on site.

Responsibility and learning

Managers are responsible for planning the work and providing the necessary resources; staff are responsible for following instructions, using equipment correctly and reporting hazards. Near misses and accidents are recorded and investigated with the aim of preventing them from happening again. After a serious incident, work may not resume until the risk has been reassessed and preventive measures reviewed.

Responsibility and review

The managing director is responsible for this document and for making sure its content is presented to everyone working on the company's behalf. It is reviewed when there are significant changes to the work, equipment or rules, after incidents, and otherwise regularly in line with the scale of the business.
