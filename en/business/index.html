Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you work as a subcontractor?

Yes. We take on excavation and reinstatement as a subcontractor for main contractors, and also work directly for clients such as property companies and municipalities.

How is the work priced?

Either a fixed quote for a defined work package or an hourly rate, depending on the job. The price is confirmed once we have gone through the documents and the site conditions.

What needs to be in place before digging?

Drawings or a specification, a confirmed scope and up-to-date utility information from the utility companies. We go through the utility information before digging starts.

Where do you work?

In the Greater Reykjavík area.
